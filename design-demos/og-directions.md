## The Six Candidates

### Candidate 1 · Best Designer A: Paula Scher (Pentagram)
- **Logic & Source:** Paula Scher's architectural typography for the Public Theater and high-impact American cultural posters.
- **Borrowing:** Oversized bold type layered with physical product, tilted label ribbons, high visual impact that grabs the viewer in 0.5 seconds.
- **Hero Material:** `assets/can-original-hires.png` with dynamic 10-point starburst and tilted badge stamps.
- **Skeleton:** Left-heavy bold typography with right-anchored towering product silhouette.
- **Grit / Boldness:** Scale contrast between giant typographic blocks and the product can.
- **Form Origin:** Carbonated cola burst and bold American pop culture energy.

### Candidate 2 · Best Designer B: Studio Dumbar / COLLINS
- **Logic & Source:** Studio Dumbar and COLLINS kinetic identity systems (precision geometry, rhythmic pacing).
- **Borrowing:** Concentric sharp geometric lines, mathematical alignment, high-tech FMCG precision.
- **Hero Material:** Triple can lineup across a precise 3-column spatial grid.
- **Skeleton:** Symmetrical 3-column flavor showcase with clean baseline typography.
- **Grit / Boldness:** Treating a commercial soda like high-performance engineering.
- **Form Origin:** The scientific precision of the 4-vitamin infusion formula.

### Candidate 3 · Real Reference A: Liquid Death / Red Bull High-Impact Social Cards
- **Logic & Source:** Liquid Death and Red Bull dark-mode social link cards with dramatic lighting.
- **Borrowing:** Deep dark field, crisp edge rim highlights, high-contrast badges, gritty unapologetic posture.
- **Hero Material:** `assets/can-extra-caffeine-crop.png` flanked by the full trio on a reflective dark pedestal.
- **Skeleton:** Centered dark heroic product stage with top brand header and bottom nutritional telemetry.
- **Grit / Boldness:** Near-black background for an effervescent sunny cola brand.
- **Form Origin:** The "Grind Vibes" extra-caffeine intensity and evening urban drive.

### Candidate 4 · Real Reference B: Modern Functional Beverage (Olipop / Poppi / Sanzo)
- **Logic & Source:** Modern DTC functional sodas dominating Instagram and TikTok feed previews.
- **Borrowing:** Friendly optimistic pastel warmth, modular ingredient stickers, high-clarity nutritional callouts.
- **Hero Material:** Can trio surrounded by floating ingredient pills (Vitamins A, C, B9, B12, Stevia Leaf).
- **Skeleton:** Modular sticker grid with soft rounded geometry and crisp contrast badges.
- **Grit / Boldness:** Honest ingredient transparency as the primary visual hero.
- **Form Origin:** 100% stevia sweetening, zero sugar, and zero calories.

### Candidate 5 · Roulette Web #13: Bold Geometric Constructivism
- **Logic & Source:** Constructivist diagonal split planes and geometric collision.
- **Borrowing:** 12-degree diagonal section divider, bold contrasting ground colors, sharp chevron accents.
- **Hero Material:** Hero can cutting through a razor-sharp color seam between Off-White and Vibrant Orange.
- **Skeleton:** Diagonal bipartite split (text left, dynamic angled product right).
- **Grit / Boldness:** Diagonal cut slashing through the entire 1200×630 frame.
- **Form Origin:** VIVE's signature angled section cuts (`.cut-down`, `.cut-both`).

### Candidate 6 · Roulette East #9: High-Density Graphic Typographic Broadside
- **Logic & Source:** Japanese beverage packaging and urban convenience store poster typography.
- **Borrowing:** High information density, stacked multi-colored graphic badges, textured price bursts.
- **Hero Material:** Original can surrounded by high-tempo graphic stamps (₱67, Lawson, 7-Eleven, SM).
- **Skeleton:** Perimeter-framed broadside with dense typographic borders and central product icon.
- **Grit / Boldness:** Dense information architecture without clutter.
- **Form Origin:** Philippine retail landscape (Lawson, 7-Eleven, SM, Ayala Malls).

## The Three Final Selected Directions

1. **Direction 1: "The Explosive Starburst" (Candidate 1 + Brand Direction C)**
   - **Ground:** Full Vibrant VIVE Orange (`#F27A21`).
   - **DNA:** 10-point sun starburst behind the original can, tilted navy labels, bold pop energy.
   - **Atmosphere:** Optimistic, explosive, maximum stop-power in feeds.

2. **Direction 2: "The Midnight Trio Grid" (Candidate 2 & 3: Precision Architectural Lineup)**
   - **Ground:** Deep Midnight Ink (`#14183F` / `#2B3990`).
   - **DNA:** Symmetrical trio can lineup on an angled reflective pedestal, crisp modernist typography, 4-vitamin badges.
   - **Atmosphere:** Sophisticated, premium, functional beverage authority.

3. **Direction 3: "The Bold Street Slant" (Candidate 5 + Brand Direction A)**
   - **Ground:** 12-degree diagonal split: Crisp Off-White (`#F9F9F9`) into Electric Orange (`#F27A21`).
   - **DNA:** Aggressive angled typographic lockup, speed lines, halftone dot accents, ₱67 star sticker, athletic swagger.
   - **Atmosphere:** Dynamic, streetwear-infused, fast-moving.

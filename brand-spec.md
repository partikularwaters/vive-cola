## Visual language (client's words, 2026-11-14)
Sharp and energetic. Vibrant and optimistic. Intense but sweet. Cheerful. Dynamic and motivating. Charged and playful.
- Signature shapes: the **10-point star** and **triangles**.
- Shape asset library: See [source/deck-extract/README.md](source/deck-extract/README.md) and [source/deck-extract/showcase.html](source/deck-extract/showcase.html) for the full 70-item library of vector SVGs and high-resolution PNGs across all brand colors.
- Reading for the site: sharp edges and angled cuts over soft rounded pills; motion that feels charged (quick, snappy) rather than floaty.

## Product facts
- VIVE, "The vitamin-charged cola." Lead with the vitamins; stevia is secondary reassurance. Sweetened with 100% stevia. Zero calories. 12 oz can. ₱67.
- Product line (client, 2026-11-16):
  - **VIVE Classic:** "Your dose of good vibes." The original orange can. Vitamins A, B9, B12, and C.
    - Nutrition facts from the Classic label, per 12 fl oz can: 0 calories, 0 g fat, 25 mg sodium (1%), 0 g carbohydrate, 0 g sugar, 0 g protein; vitamin A 10%, B9 (folate) 25%, B12 50%, and C 50% of the daily value; caffeine 34 mg.
  - **VIVE Glow:** "Your dose of radiant vibes." Vitamins A, B7, C, and E, which support skin health.
  - **VIVE Focus:** "Your dose of sharp vibes." Vitamins B6, B9, B12, and C, which support mental performance.
- Retired deck-era variants (do not promote): Decaffeinated ("Your dose of relaxing vibes") and Extra Caffeine ("Your dose of grind vibes").
- CTA lines: "Grab a VIVE!" / "Spread the vibes!" Retailers: Lawson, 7-Eleven, SM, and Ayala Malls.
- Shop catalog and sample prices (prototype, pending client confirmation): single cans ₱67; Trio sampler ₱195; Classic six-pack ₱380; 12-can party pack ₱720; baseball jersey ₱1,299; running shirt and shorts ₱1,499; bomber jacket ₱1,899; canvas tote ₱449; coaster set ₱299; cap ₱599; A5 notebook ₱249; pen set ₱149; sticker pack ₱99; tumbler ₱699; ceramic mug ₱399.
- Name: from the last letters of *revive* and *survive* (vive-copy.md). The middle "!" / IV marks the four vitamins.

How VIVE got started

VIVE started with a group of friends unwinding after a long day at work, sharing a few colas and good vibes. Talk turned to health and vitamins: how hard it is to take supplements every day, how easy it is to forget a dose, and how confusing it is to pick the right brand.

Then one friend looked at the cola in his hand and asked, What if this cola had vitamins in it? That simple question sparked the idea, and VIVE was born.

The name VIVE comes directly from the ending letters of revive and survive. It captures the daily reset people need between morning focus, afternoon deadlines, and evening sports.

On every can, the middle letters stand out as the Roman numeral IV. That marks the four vitamins in each recipe. VIVE Classic carries vitamins A, B9, B12, and C for everyday energy. VIVE Glow carries vitamins A, B7, C, and E to support skin health. VIVE Focus carries vitamins B6, B9, B12, and C to support mental performance.

All three keep the cola taste you know. We sweeten them with 100% stevia, so every can has zero calories.

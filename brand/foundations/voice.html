Voice

Lead with the vitamins, keep stevia as reassurance, and write plain, upbeat lines.

VIVE sounds sharp, energetic, and cheerful. Lead with the four vitamins and zero sugar, the two benefits the Brand Guidelines ask every piece to make easy to spot. Stevia and zero calories are the reassurance that follows.

Product facts

Every can is 12 oz, ₱67, sweetened with 100% stevia, and has zero sugar and zero calories. VIVE is sold at Lawson, 7-Eleven, SM, and Ayala Malls.

Rules

  • Write short, declarative sentences with a concrete benefit.
  • Use the Oxford comma in every list of three or more.
  • Avoid contrast reveals ("not X, but Y"), negation lists ("no X, no Y, no Z"), rhetorical questions, and buzzwords such as "elevate" or "game-changer."
  • Never promote the retired deck-era variants (Decaffeinated and Extra Caffeine).

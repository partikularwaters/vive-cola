# VIVE Website Router Poster — Six Candidates & Direction Cards

**Canvas Specification:** 1-Page Poster (A4 Portrait: 595.28pt × 841.89pt / 210mm × 297mm)  
**Destination URL:** `https://vive-cola.vercel.app` (Global 100% Click Target)  
**Brand:** VIVE (Vitamin-Charged Cola)  
**Brand Colors:** VIVE Orange (`#F27A21`), Deep Navy (`#2B3990` / `#1D2766`), Sun Accent (`#F4BD6A`), Clean Off-White (`#F9F9F9`), Deep Ink (`#14183F`)  
**Key Typography:** Montserrat Bold (900/800), Montserrat Regular (Google Fonts)  
**Official Retailers:** Lawson, 7-Eleven, SM Supermalls, Ayala Malls  

---

## Spec & Requirements

1. **Routing Mechanism:** Every point on the PDF page must function as an interactive link directly to `https://vive-cola.vercel.app`.
2. **Brand Resonance:** The poster must immediately communicate VIVE's identity: vitamin-charged energy, 100% stevia sweetening, 0 calories, vibrant orange ground, signature 10-point stars, and energetic angle cuts.
3. **Reproducibility:** Governed by an automated mechanical script (`scripts/build_router_pdf.py`) that exports the source HTML to PDF predictably and synchronizes with current website styles and assets.

## The Six Candidates

### Candidate 1 · Best Designer A: Paula Scher (Pentagram)
- **Logic & Source:** Paula Scher's dynamic architectural typography and explosive cultural identity posters.
- **Borrowing:** Intersecting angled color bars, massive tilted headline ribbons, and tight typographic lockups framing a central heroic product.
- **Hero Material:** Flagship VIVE Classic Can (`assets/can-classic-web.webp`), 10-point sun starburst (`assets/shapes/star-10pt-sun.svg`), and floating vitamin stamps (A, B9, B12, C).
- **Skeleton:** Central hero burst with angled top header banner and a high-contrast deep navy bottom launch bar.
- **Audacity:** Tilted headline blocks overlapping the starburst with zero visual compromise.
- **Form Origin:** The effervescent, carbonated soda explosion and 10-point starburst from the brand identity deck.

### Candidate 2 · Best Designer B: Experimental Jetset / Swiss Structuralism
- **Logic & Source:** Experimental Jetset's high-contrast grid layouts and stark typographic hierarchy.
- **Borrowing:** Oversized digital URL (`VIVE-COLA.VERCEL.APP`) forming the structural grid border, extreme scale contrast between micro-telemetry and macro-letterforms.
- **Hero Material:** Classic Can anchored between rigid geometric planes of solid Navy and VIVE Orange.
- **Skeleton:** Strict modular grid with top identity zone, central product strip, and bottom digital telemetry.
- **Audacity:** Making the actual web address the primary graphic boundary.
- **Form Origin:** Digital portal nature of the router document.

### Candidate 3 · Real Reference A: Liquid Death / Red Bull High-Impact Key Visuals
- **Logic & Source:** Modern high-energy beverage posters with cinematic splash, ice, and dynamic depth.
- **Borrowing:** Dynamic cola splash (`assets/cola-splash.png`), tumbling ice cubes (`assets/ice-cube.png`), and dramatic drop shadows that pop off the page.
- **Hero Material:** Classic can flanked by effervescent splashing cola and faceted ice.
- **Skeleton:** Centered explosive visual hero with floating benefit callouts and a luminous interactive button.
- **Audacity:** Saturated physical liquid splash bursting through clean graphic geometry.
- **Form Origin:** The refreshing sensory thirst of chilled vitamin cola.

### Candidate 4 · Real Reference B: Modern Functional Beverage (Olipop / Poppi / Sanzo)
- **Logic & Source:** Modern DTC functional sodas presenting their complete family range on retail posters.
- **Borrowing:** Triple-can lineup (Classic, Glow, Focus), clean color blocking with a diagonal seam, and clear flavor benefit ribbons.
- **Hero Material:** Cans of Classic (`can-classic-web.webp`), Glow (`can-glow-web.webp`), and Focus (`can-focus-web.webp`) standing in family alignment.
- **Skeleton:** Diagonal bipartite split (navy top to off-white/orange base) with 3-column flavor badges and interactive URL footer.
- **Audacity:** Presenting all three flavor personalities on a single executive router page.
- **Form Origin:** The newly expanded 3-variant line approved on 2026-11-16.

### Candidate 5 · Second Roulette #14: Graphic Pop & Angled Label Energy
- **Logic & Source:** Retro-futuristic pop art soda advertising with bold sticker geometry.
- **Borrowing:** Saturated sun-yellow ground with navy border, angled sticker labels ("0 SUGAR", "100% STEVIA", "4 VITAMINS"), and stamped pricing/nutrition seals.

### Candidate 6 · Second Roulette #7: Editorial Broadside & Soda Billboard
- **Logic & Source:** American mid-century broadside soda billboard reimagined for modern digital screens.
- **Borrowing:** Heavy display typography, framed borders, nutritional fact stamp, and authentic retail store badges (Lawson, 7-Eleven, SM, Ayala Malls).

   - *Logic:* Candidate 1 + Candidate 3 (Paula Scher Typography + Beverage Key Visual Splash).
   - *Palette:* Vibrant VIVE Orange ground (`#F27A21`), Sun Star (`#F4BD6A`), Deep Navy launchpad (`#1D2766`).

2. **Direction 2: "The Flavor Trio" (Complete Lineup & Clean Digital Portal)**
   - *Logic:* Candidate 4 (Modern DTC Family Lineup + Architectural Clean Cut).

   - *Form Origin:* The full 3-variant product family and Direction C's diagonal section cuts.

3. **Direction 3: "Editorial Billboard" (High-Voltage Typographic Angle)**
   - *Logic:* Candidate 5 + Candidate 6 (Direction A Angled Billboard + Retail Authenticity).
   - *Palette:* Saturated Orange with massive tilted Navy typography, sun hazard cuts, and dark footer.
   - *Hero:* Giant angled Classic can breaking out of stacked typographic banners ("VITAMIN-CHARGED COLA"), authentic retailer logos at the base, and stamped official digital access seal.
   - *Form Origin:* Direction A's tilted headers taken to giant poster scale.

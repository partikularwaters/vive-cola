# VIVE Christmas Poster Design Directions

Date: 2026-11-16  
Brief: Christmas Theme Poster featuring VIVE Original Cola and Filipino Lechon Belly for Noche Buena.  
Format: Vertical Poster (1200×1700 px).  
Source Assets: `assets/shapes/triangle-acute-orange.svg` (turned Christmas green), `assets/shapes/star-10pt-tilted-12deg-sun.svg` (Sun Yellow `#F4BD6A`), `assets/can-original-hires.png`, `assets/lechon-belly-placeholder.png`, `assets/logo-vive-navy-tagline.png`, and `assets/retailers.png`.

---

## Direction 1 · Challenger Festive Feast (Bold VIVE Orange Blockbuster)

- **Logic & Source:** Commercial Challenger Brand Blockbuster (inspired by bold beverage street advertising, Oatly & Liquid Death holiday posters).
- **Palette:** VIVE Orange (`#F27A21`) primary ground, Pine Green (`#134E2E` / `#1B5E3A`) tree, Sun Yellow (`#F4BD6A`) star and accents, VIVE Navy (`#2B3990`) contrast ribbons, and Off-White (`#F9F9F9`).
- **Tree & Star:** Towering acute green tree zoomed in at the center, apex anchored at Y=330px. Sun Yellow 10-point star with a +12° tilt centered directly on the tree apex, radiating subtle geometric festive starburst rays.
- **Headline:** Direction A tilted banners: "NOCHE BUENA DRINK?" in a Navy angled ribbon, and "WE GOTCHA!" in crisp White with a high-contrast shadow cut.
- **Foreground Duo:** Crispy golden-orange lechon belly on the left and tall VIVE can on the right with icy splash accents, casting grounded contact shadows.
- **Branding:** VIVE logo at top left, "₱67 ONLY" holiday burst badge, and retailers strip at bottom right.

## Direction 2 · Midnight Noche Buena (Atmospheric Deep Ink & Golden Warmth)

- **Palette:** Deep Midnight Navy (`#0D1026` to `#1D2766`), Evergreen Pine (`#0F4C2A`), Sun Yellow (`#F4BD6A`), Warm Off-White (`#F9F9F9`), and Golden Glow (`#FFD182`).
- **Logic & Source:** Authentic Philippine Noche Buena tradition. Families celebrate at midnight; the deep blue-ink night sky makes the festive golden glow, evergreen tree, and roasted lechon pop with warmth.
- **Tree & Star:** Rich evergreen acute tree with delicate geometric fairy-light particles and sun particle scatter. The tilted 10-point star sits at the apex, emitting a warm circular holiday glow.
- **Headline:** Clean, monumental display typography: "Noche Buena drink?" in tracked Off-White Montserrat Bold, and "WE GOTCHA!" in towering Sun Yellow Montserrat 900.
- **Foreground Duo:** Glistening roasted lechon belly roll on a dark wood presentation board with banana leaf trim on the left; frosty, cold VIVE can on the right.
- **Branding:** White VIVE logo with gold tagline at top left, glowing white retailers ribbon at bottom right.

---

## Direction 3 · Dynamic Diagonal Cut (Pop-Art Holiday Split)

- **Palette:** 50/50 Diagonal Split — VIVE Navy (`#2B3990`) upper sky transitioning into VIVE Orange (`#F27A21`) lower ground at -14°.
- **Logic & Source:** Contemporary Pop-Art Street Poster (inspired by Paula Scher & Studio Dumbar angled typography).
- **Tree & Star:** Vibrant emerald green acute tree cutting through the diagonal color split, bridging night sky and holiday feast. Capped by the Sun 10-point tilted star at the apex.
- **Headline:** High-energy graphic badge lockup: "Noche Buena drink?" inside an angled white sticker ribbon, and "WE GOTCHA!" in a massive Sun Yellow burst shape.
- **Foreground Duo:** Sticker-popped lechon belly roll on the left, angled VIVE can with flying ice cubes and effervescent bubbles on the right.
- **Branding:** VIVE logo top left on an angled tag, retailers strip bottom right inside a sharp angled footer bar.
